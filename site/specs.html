Research annex · Companion to the reference architecture

The specifications, with the research behind them

Each of the fifteen specifications, assessed the way an implementing institution would assess it: what it does, the published research that bears on it, the threat and privacy models that literature has explored, and what a production implementation should therefore do.

Method and provenance. Sources were located and verified in August 2026 and are labelled by type: peer-reviewed venues, standards documents, preprints, and industry analysis. Preprints and industry sources are included where they are the best available treatment of an engineering question, and are labelled so they can be weighted accordingly. Links go to the publisher or archive. This annex is educational material, not legal or procurement advice.
